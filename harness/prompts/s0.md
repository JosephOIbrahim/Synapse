=== THE FOUR QUESTIONS ===

**Q1 — What does Houdini 22 ship natively?**
This is the floor SYNAPSE sits above, and it moved in H22. Read SideFX's own material: release notes, the H22 journal, documentation for any AI/agent/assistant feature, the native rig-generation tooling. **Primary sources only** — sidefx.com, their docs, their release notes. What does it do, what does it cost, what does it NOT do?

**Q2 — What else exists in this space?**
Houdini AI Assistant, houdini2chat, MCP-based Houdini tools, anything else reachable. For each: what is the integration shape (in-process or external), what is the pricing model, what do users actually say. **A vendor's own claim is REPORTED, never OBSERVED** — including where it flatters us.

**Q3 — What kills tool adoption in a VFX pipeline?** *(this is the most valuable question)*
Not "what do artists like." What gets a tool REMOVED after a trial. Look for: post-mortems, pipeline-TD writing, studio blog posts, conference talks, forum threads with specifics. The failure modes that matter are usually operational rather than functional — a tool that breaks a farm submission, corrupts a cache, needs a per-seat licence dance, or cannot be rolled back mid-show.

**Q4 — What is a mid-sized studio's actual constraint?**
Seat count, licence budget, pipeline-TD headcount, whether they have a dedicated pipeline team at all, how they evaluate new tools, who decides. **A tool adopted by a 12-artist shop is a different product from one adopted by a 400-artist shop**, and SYNAPSE's positioning names mid-size specifically.
