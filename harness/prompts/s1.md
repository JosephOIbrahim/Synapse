=== WHAT THIS IS ===

**What does SYNAPSE actually DO today, per tool, mapped to a real artist task?**

Not what it registers. Not what its docstring promises. What an artist could sit down and use this afternoon.

This week found five Solaris tools that were registered, tested, and completely unreachable; three COP tools that build node topology and never cook; and a knowledge corpus five years stale. **The gap between "a tool exists" and "a tool works" is this project's most reliable finding**, and this leg measures it deliberately rather than discovering it again.

=== THE CLASSIFICATION ===

Every registered tool gets exactly one:

```
WORKS        exercised end-to-end and produced the right artefact. Say how you
             know - a test, a live run, an anchor.
PARTIAL      does something real, with a named gap. State the gap.
SCAFFOLD     builds structure and does not execute. reaction_diffusion,
             pixel_sort and bake_textures self-report as this; find the others.
UNREACHABLE  registered but no path invokes it. The Solaris family was this for
             an entire phase and its tests passed against a mock.
UNKNOWN      could not be exercised here. Say what would be needed.
```

**SCAFFOLD and UNREACHABLE are the two that matter.** A tool list is a sales document; a tool list with those two marked is an engineering document.

=== THE SECOND AXIS, WHICH IS THE POINT ===

For each WORKS or PARTIAL tool: **what artist task does it serve, and would an artist reach for it?**

A tool can work perfectly and serve nothing. `cops_reaction_diffusion` is a Gray-Scott solver; a lighting TD on a deadline has never wanted one. `synapse_solaris_assemble_chain` might save twenty minutes on every shot setup. **Those are the same line in a feature list and completely different products.**

Group by what an artist is actually doing: scene assembly, look-dev, lighting, rendering, comp, caching, debugging. Note which groups are thin — the gaps say more than the coverage.
