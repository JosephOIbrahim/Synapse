=== THE REDESIGN, FROM ITS ORGANIZING IDEA ===

The study's thesis, and the thing to build toward:

  **The work is the product. The panel's only job is to make it trustworthy
  enough to walk away from.**

Three moves follow from that:

**1. THE MARK CARRIES STATE.** The wordmark's leading glyph is the halt affordance. Idle = outline. Working = filling. Done = filled. An artist cannot have activity on screen without the stop being visible, because they are the same object.

  R17 wanted an always-visible emergency halt; R29 found `_on_stop` already
  exists and is HONEST — it aborts the loop cooperatively and refuses to claim
  idle, printing "Stopping — waiting on <tool>…" until the worker actually
  stops. **DO NOT REPLACE `_on_stop`.** Its state-gating is correct: a Stop shown
  when nothing runs is the same lie as a consent gate that does not gate (R18).
  The mark REFLECTS that existing state; it does not become a second Stop.

**2. THE RESULT IS THE HERO.** Today the panel is a chat log with a status bar. The study inverts it: when work completes, the RESULT occupies the frame — the render, the graph, the diff — and the chrome recedes. Everything else is a quiet frame around it.

**3. CREDITED DECISIONS.** Every mutation states what it chose AND why, in the frame, the way a project credits its named partner. `Dark_Glass — over Diamond, closer to scene IOR`. Not a log line; a caption.
