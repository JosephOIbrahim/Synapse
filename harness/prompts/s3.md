=== THE QUESTION, HONESTLY STATED ===

Joe asked how to make SYNAPSE *"so novel to mid-sized VFX production per Houdini artist that it is an irrefutable positively viewed tool."*

**"Irrefutable" is not achievable and pretending otherwise would waste this leg.** Artists reject good tools for bad reasons and keep bad tools for good ones. What IS achievable, and what you are deriving a plan toward:

```
LEGIBLE VALUE       an artist can tell, in ONE session, what it did for them
SURVIVABLE FAILURE  when it is wrong the shot is not lost, and they know immediately
NO NEW BURDEN       nothing extra to remember, configure, or babysit
```

A tool with all three gets adopted. **A tool missing the second is uninstalled after one incident, however good the first is** — and S2 will have told you exactly what that incident looks like.

=== THE RULE THAT MAKES THIS A PLAN AND NOT A PITCH ===

**Every recommendation traces to a finding.** Format, per item, no exceptions:

```
DO          the specific thing
BECAUSE     the S0/S1/S2 finding that forces it, cited
INSTEAD OF  the plausible alternative this beats, and why
COSTS       rough, honestly
FALSIFIED   the observation that would prove this wrong
```

**If a recommendation has no finding behind it, it does not go in the plan.** It goes in a section titled "believed, unverified" — which is legal, useful, and must be labelled (the harness's tier rule).

The FALSIFIED line is not decoration. A plan whose items cannot be wrong is a wish list, and this project has spent five days establishing what that costs.

=== THE THREE QUESTIONS THE PLAN MUST ANSWER ===

**1. What is the ONE thing an artist notices in the first session?**
Not the feature list. The single moment where the value is undeniable. In-process access means SYNAPSE can do things an outside-in tool structurally cannot — S1 will have found which of those actually work today. **If nothing qualifies, say so.** That is the most important possible finding and it reframes everything downstream.

**2. What must be true before a pipeline TD signs off?**
The TD decides, not the artist. S2 will have their objections. **The plan answers them or names them as accepted risk** — a plan that omits the decision-maker's veto is not a plan.

**3. What is the smallest credible pilot?**
Not a launch. **One artist, one show, one bounded task, one quarter.** What is the task, what is the success criterion, and what is the pre-agreed abort condition? SYNAPSE has ZERO production users and every claim about adoption is currently ASSUMED — the pilot exists to convert the largest UNKNOWN in S2's list into an observation.
