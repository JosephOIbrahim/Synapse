design(panel): the four subtractions that needed no ruling, and a ranked backlog

Three design review rounds (2026-09-05, -09-14, -09-15) were each internally
ordered and never ranked against each other, so the newest findings sat behind
two older rounds that were themselves waiting. harness/design_review/RANKED.md
is that cross-round order. The headline it carries: of 28 open items, 22 need a
word from the Creative Director and 6 need hands. This is a decision backlog,
not a build backlog, and nothing at any priority in the first group can be
started by anyone until it is ruled.

This commit applies the four from the newest round that carry no design call.

FLAT ROOT. The root painted a gradient of PANEL +/- ATMOSPHERE_DELTA -- 8 levels
out of 255 spread over 760px, about one level per 95 pixels. The argument for it
was that a tall pane should not read as dead vinyl, but an effect below the
perceptual floor cannot do that job, and it still cost a gradient on the widget
that repaints most. It is the one subtraction BOTH rendered directions agreed
on, which is why it needs no ruling. atmosphere() stays in tokens.py: it is a
sound utility with no consumer today, and deleting a function to prove a point
is not a cleanup.

TRANSCRIPT DOCUMENT MARGIN. Qt's QTextDocument ships a 4px margin on all four
sides that nobody in this design system authored. At 0 the document returns 8px
of vertical space to the surface the panel exists to show, and the left content
edge moves off an anonymous Qt constant onto the design system's own GUTTER.
The margin is inside the document, not the widget, so no layout, gutter or
rhythm role moves.

GLYPH_MD / GLYPH_SM. 18 and 14 are not type -- they are the em-box a status dot
and a chevron need to sit at the right optical weight beside 12px text -- but
they were bare arithmetic in the stylesheet (SIZE_UI * 3 // 2 and * 7 // 6),
which a type census reads as two more rungs on the ramp. That is how a four-size
scale gets counted as six. Naming them changes zero pixels and states that they
scale with the UI size while sitting off the hierarchy.

FONT_FLOOR_PROVENANCE. The string said UNKNOWN and "awaiting the GUI paste" for
a measurement that had been taken -- SideFX Source Sans Pro, pixelSize 27,
pointSize 10, 192 DPI, read live and twice -- and cited the probe at a repo-root
path where the file has never existed. Both corrected; no value changed. The
floor stays 11 and stays absolute, which is now a stated position rather than an
absence of data, with the open ruling named.

DEFERRED, AND WHY IT IS NOT A KEPT ITEM

The three dead QWidget#DsHeader margin rules should go: Qt does not honour
margin on a bare QWidget selector so they paint nothing, and they perturb the
rail's measured height with an inconsistent sign across densities (airy -8,
standard 0, tight +4), which inverts the density lever.

Removing them requires listing QWidget#DsHeader in test_panel_sweep_a's
BC_WAVE_RULED_SELECTORS, and that list strips whole rule blocks BY SELECTOR from
the append-only comparison. Measured against the guard's own pattern:
QWidget#DsRoot matches 1 block, QWidget#DsHeader matches 4 -- including the live
background/hairline rule forty lines up. The exemption would have stopped
guarding a rule that still ships. Trading a real guard on a live rule for the
deletion of one that paints nothing is a bad trade; it needs a by-declaration
exemption, not a by-selector one. Nothing visible either way, since expert is
the only composable profile and the density branches cannot fire.

A GUARD CAUGHT THE PROVENANCE EDIT, CORRECTLY

test_panel_typography::test_floor_constant_has_provenance pins the first word of
that string to a controlled vocabulary -- measured | DOC-STATED | UNKNOWN -- so
the floor always declares its epistemic status in one of three known states. The
first draft of this change opened it "MEASURED", which is outside the vocabulary,
and the suite refused it. The string now uses the guard's own word rather than a
louder one. Worth recording because it is the counter-example to the rest of
today: a guard whose width exactly matched what it meant to catch, and which
therefore caught a real thing on its first exposure to it.

WHAT GUARDS THIS

QWidget#DsRoot is added to BC_WAVE_RULED_SELECTORS -- the mechanism that exists
for exactly this, a ruled change to an upstream rule -- and no further. Proven
with a negative control: an unruled upstream edit planted in the same sheet
still fails the append-only guard.

Seat suite under Houdini 22.0.400: 6 failed, 201 passed, identical to the
pre-change baseline by name, not just by count. No new red, none newly passing.

TWO CLAIMS WITHDRAWN FROM MY OWN WRITING

Both are the same failure the v5.74.0 audit caught six times today -- a claim
read out of a document and restated as an independent measurement -- and both
were caught by going to look rather than by review.

The chat_display comment said the content edge moves 50 -> 46 "via the
formatter's 16px speaker indent", taken from READABILITY.md. No such indent
could be found in message_formatter.py. The vertical 8px does not depend on that
figure and is verified here; the exact horizontal edge does, and is now marked
unverified in both the comment and RANKED.md.

RANKED.md opened "of 28 open items, 22 need a word from the Creative Director",
taken from a synthesis pass. The raw per-item verdicts say 40 calls checked: 31
OPEN, 6 SHIPPED, 2 PARTIAL, 1 SUPERSEDED. The 28/22 is that pass consolidating
cross-document duplicates, which is a reasonable thing to do and is not a thing
I checked. The file now states the verified 31-of-40 and attributes the
consolidation as unverified. The shape of the finding does not change; its
provenance does.
