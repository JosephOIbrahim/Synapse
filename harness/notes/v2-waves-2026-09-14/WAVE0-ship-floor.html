Moneta — the root question

The answer is none of the three shapes the question offered, and none of the fourth either.

The answer: none of the three shapes

Moneta binds, writes durably, and reads back after restart. Say this forcefully, because the most expensive mistake available in this report is rewriting a storage layer that is already correct:

The Moneta durability mechanism is sound. There is no persistence bug to fix. Do not rewrite moneta_store.py, durability.py, or the deposit path on the belief that deposits are being lost.
