release: v5.79.1 -- the transcript stopped shouting

v5.79.0 shipped a chat transcript that rendered EVERY message in capitals at
weight 500. The speaker-label pass selected BlockUnderCursor and merged its label
font across the whole block, and the label, the timestamp and the message body
share one block. Six of six fragments came back AllUppercase, measured.

Joe found it in the live panel: "the chat text in SYNAPSE is all caps and tightly
spaced. That makes it hard for neurodivergent users to read."

Product: the label format reaches the speaker run only and the caps transform is
gone, which also un-bolds the body (it keeps WEIGHT_REGULAR instead of inheriting
the label's 500); CHAT_LEADING_PT 0.75 -> 1.50, taking the line step from 17px to
18px, a ratio of 1.42x -> exactly the 1.50x WCAG 1.4.12 asks; the transcript's own
turn gaps 24 -> 48 and 8 -> 16, shared keys untouched; _MEASURE_CHARS 66 -> 73 so
the column goes 462px -> 511px; stranded ** markers stripped; and the model's
system prompt now asks for sentence case with no em dashes.

Pinned by tests/panel/test_transcript_readability.py, which asserts the RENDERED
document because that is where the defect lived. No test had ever done so, which
is why it shipped. Four of five redden under a deliberate break.

Both open panel rulings closed. The caps fix largely dissolved the cpl-band
problem: the column had been calibrated against capitals, and the narrow dock now
reads 53.7 cpl IN BAND where it was 44.4 and unreachable. Ceiling ruled 75 -> 85,
hard red unchanged at 90.

Two bugs introduced while fixing this, both caught and named in the notes: a lazy
import that deadlocked the seat suite against the ws_bridge thread, and a test
that segfaulted Shiboken by reading QTextLayout after other tests walked the doc.

Verified: stock suite 9624 passed / 0 failed; panel gate GREEN on master with both
ratchets holding; measure probe exits 0 with 3 of 4 corners in band.
