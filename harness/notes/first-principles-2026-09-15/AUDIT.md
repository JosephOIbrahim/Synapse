## What the graph itself got wrong

1. **P1 as the sole deletion criterion** (the run's author already suspects this). Right about content — all five `+0 -1` — and blind to procedure: every one of the five is checked out in a OneDrive worktree that `worktree remove` refuses without `--force`, and `branch -D` refuses while checked out. The suspected "uncommitted work" turned out to be a stale copy of a committed blob plus symlink noise — but the graph could not have known that without looking, and it did not look. The +326 uncommitted lines it also did not look for sit in a different worktree (m2-pgdrm).
2. **P2 was the wrong framing for the right verdict.** "42 shared files" is the state file's supersession-edge label; the triager actually proved ancestry. The label invites the names≠content fallacy the verdict did not commit — and then the plan bakes in a gate (`--is-ancestor`) that stops working after the very rebase the plan orders next (P9).
3. **SUPERSEDED covers three evidentiary standards**: byte-identical-on-master (windows-installer, 21/25), intent-covered-by-different-code (ledger 4/8, h1 4/4, panel-ux 3/3) and superseded-by-another-branch (tops, 42/42 differ from master). One label, one confidence band (0.88–0.97), no way to tell them apart from the verdict alone.
4. **ARCHIVE_DELETE on unreplicated content is a ruling, not a triage.** stray (826 lines) and goalposts (2 commits) were both marked delete on a judgment of worthlessness the evidence cannot carry; the refuter caught one, not the other.
5. **Action steps addressed to the wrong actor.** Merges, pushes, push-deletes and tags are written as commands while the state file lists them as human gates. On a PUBLIC origin a push-delete is the most permanent step in the plan and appears in five verdicts as a one-liner; two of those target refs that do not exist on origin.
6. **The inventory frame excluded worktree state and the stash.** Branch tips were triaged; the estate also holds worktree dirt (one real: m2-pgdrm +326/-1) and `stash@{0}` (trivial, but uninventoried).
7. **The tool-count literal is hand-edited in three branches** and only one of its two homes is pinned (`CLAUDE.md:3` by `tests/test_phase0c_doc1_toolcount.py:41`; `README.md:121` unpinned). A number that must be computed is being typed.
8. **P6 for the artist lenses.** "Registered" (128) is neither what the panel offers (134) nor what the worker filters to by default (98); any lens finding that counts registry entries as artist-reachable tools counts the wrong surface.
9. **The conductor's own context propagated an error** (P12): "NOT on origin" derived from missing upstream config, not from the remote. One checker built a correction on it. Every future context block should derive origin presence from `ls-remote`.
10. **`refuted=true` is overloaded**: for feat/scene-model it meant "one citation fabricated, verdict unchanged"; for goalposts it meant "verdict changed". A downstream reader cannot tell which from the flag.
