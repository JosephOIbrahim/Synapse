## B · Approve the release (vNEXT tag) — ~25 min, at the rig, any day

**2.** Say: **ritual**
   → Driver preps: reads this morning's v5.50.0 ritual (the observed precedent), greens every
   automatable gate, then queues YOUR acts one at a time. You do nothing yet.

**3.** Your hands, one at a time — say **pass** after each and the driver records it:

   · **g5** — lifecycle (open Houdini, use /synapse, close clean)
   · **g6** — core smoke  · remaining g's as the driver calls them
   · **g9** — rollback

     Counts twice: W5-UNDO-GUI receipt + the release's marquee demo.

**4.** **drop.json** — yours alone. You dictate, Claude types as instrument (the recorded
   precedent in the file itself), or you edit it by hand. Either way: your words, on record.

**5.** Three closing words, in order, one each:
   **bump** (six version surfaces) → **verify** (R.R must read full green) → **tag**
   (tag + GitHub Release, notes already drafted in RELEASE-DRAFT-vNEXT.md).

*You'll see:* each R.R gate flip pending→pass as you go; the last verify reads READY; the tag
lands with your notes. If any gate stays red, the ritual STOPS there — no partial releases.
