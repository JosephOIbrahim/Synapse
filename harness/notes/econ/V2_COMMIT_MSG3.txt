fix(econ): V2 - the adversarial pass found a blocker in the gate itself

Six hostile read-only lenses over the committed leg, every finding then handed to
an independent verifier told to REFUTE it. 36 verdicts, 27 upheld, 9 refuted.
Article IV: the crucible never built what it attacks.

V2-F10 BLOCKER. VoiceGate.submit returned an ACCEPTED outcome without recording
it, so resolve() fell through to the object's ORIGINAL free field. The canonical
loop - reject, the model rewrites, the gate accepts - rendered the string the gate
had just REJECTED, violations intact. Four verifiers reproduced it independently.

The invariant-8 arm that should have caught it was blind BY FIXTURE: the test
built its gate around a verdict already carrying the good sentence and submitted
that same sentence, so "returns the accepted text" and "returns the pre-gate text"
were equal by construction. Law 1 in my own suite - a check that could not fail
for the defect it was written to catch. The fixture now carries prose the gate
must reject.

submit() latches acceptance as it latches the floor; resolve() RAISES rather than
answering before anything is settled; `settled` (a verdict is final) is now a
different question from `exhausted` (the floor fired), because conflating them is
what let the accept path escape.
