    None is the honest state today.

THE VOICE CONTRACT
  Ten mechanical rules on the one free field, each demonstrated firing AND silent
  on a conforming verdict; the firing-case set is asserted equal to the rule set,
  so a rule cannot ship undemonstrated. Three rejections and the field is
  abandoned for a verdict templated from `decision` - validated by the same rules
  it protects, so a quoted hedge degrades the template instead of riding through
  the gate it just failed. Nothing structured to compose from -> empty, never a
  plausible substitute.
