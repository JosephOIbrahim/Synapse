harness(ECON-01): E1 - the tool surface, decomposed

A total is not a plan. T.0 published 17,310 for the mcp_http surface; E1
splits that same payload per tool, per component, and the decomposition
changes three of the brief's four premises.

The reader is calibrated before it is trusted (R60): it rebuilds the
registry as committed at T.0's own commit and reproduces 115 tools /
17,310 tokens exactly. All five readers are mutation-tested, 8/8 controls
flip (R133). C4b, the paired negative control, failed on first authoring
and is recorded rather than quietly fixed - the control was wrong, not
the reader.

  DRIFT     17,310 was measured at 115 tools. The registry now holds 120
            and the live surface is 18,962. The +5 are the Solaris tools
            S1 found unreachable and SR1 M1/M2/M5 repaired (+1,652).

  SHAPE     schema 56.8%, auto-generated annotations 21.0%, ALL tool
            descriptions 18.1%, names 5.2%. The wire carries four
            components, not three. Derived metadata outweighs every
            description in the product.

  PARETO    refuted. 37 of 120 tools carry 50% of the mass, 78 carry 80%,
            the heaviest 20 carry 33.7%. This is a sweep, not an edit.

  CEILING   2,000 is NOT reachable at 120 tools. The cheapest LEGAL
            tools/list - empty descriptions, empty schemas, names only -
            is 2,919, i.e. 919 over before a word is written. Only 81
            tools fit at that floor; 13 at the current shape. The three
            verbs the ceiling was calibrated for measure 332.

  DEAD      0 structurally dead - all 120 resolve to a handler, so the S1
            condition is closed. But 34 tools (5,175 tokens, 27.3%) have
            no recorded invocation across 58,663 audit records and 1,705
            journal records. tops_* is 15 of 17, 92.6% of family mass.

  TRAP      the audit trail is blind to read-only calls by construction
            (handlers.py:531). For 32 tools "never called" was a check
            that could never have returned "called". synapse_ping is the
            worked example. The SessionJournal closes the gap.

Measures only. Proposes no cut - that is E2's, and E2 needs E0's cache
answer first, because a cache-read surface is ~1,896 effective and the
whole reduction programme demotes to a header.
