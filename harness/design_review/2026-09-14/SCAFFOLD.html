The naming half is refused as stated. The dual emit is not a mislabel; it is a designed split, and the method's own docstring at gate_widget.py:287-291 says which half goes where:

def _on_revert_clicked(self, checked=False):

At REVIEW the operation has already continued — CLAUDE.md §1.2: "Agent proposes, artist confirms (logs proposal, continues unless rejected)." "REVERT" names the outcome the artist gets, which is the right thing for a verb to name. Calling it a rejection in disguise gets the direction backwards.
