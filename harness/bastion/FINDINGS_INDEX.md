## How to read this

- **Home section = the scout's blueprint** (SLIFE=B1 ... SSHIP=B7). A finding's cross-blueprint relevance is carried in the **Class** column and the meta-findings, not by moving it out of its finder's section.
- **Status** - the librarian's dedup verdict, not the scout's:
  - **new** = an unfixed defect/observation at this site; the blueprint should ACT. (The failure *class* may be known; the unfixed instance is still new work.)
  - **known** = already fixed or documented-accepted; reference, not new work (prior anchor cited).
  - **gated** = blocked on a human DECIDE, an RFC, or an unobtainable (UNKNOWN/gui_required) measurement before it can be actioned.
- **Class** - the failure-class this instance belongs to (W6 `HARDENING-SPEC.md` ledger + standing rulings), so a blueprint sees the pattern, not just the point.
- **(v)** in the ID column = the librarian re-observed this finding FIRST-HAND (see Spot-check log). Trust is verified, never inherited.
- **Anchor** is the scout's verbatim `file:line`. A finding with anchor `MISSING` is recorded UNPROVEN (see footer).
