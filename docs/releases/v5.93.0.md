# v5.93.0 — SYNAPSE measures how a camera moves through a scene, and draws the path

**A camera's move, measured.** `synapse_spatial_path` reads how a Solaris camera
moves across the shot and how close it comes to the scene. It returns the
travel, the height, the yaw sweep, a fitted arc with its radius, a move class,
and the nearest geometry with its distance, its frame and its side of the lens.
It is read-only. It does not move the playhead, force a cook or author
anything.

A Camera LOP writes the current frame only, so the live stage holds one frame
of the move. The read evaluates the Camera LOP's own transform parameters at
every frame, and then checks the result against the composed stage at the
current frame, in position and in rotation. The result carries that check as
`stage_check`.

**UNKNOWN, with the reason.** Where one frame of the stage cannot vouch for the
other frames, the move comes back UNKNOWN and says why. That covers a camera no
Camera LOP authors, or that more than one does; look-at switched on; a camera
that already had a transform before the Camera LOP edited it; an animated
parent; a retime after the camera; extra transform operations added downstream;
and a composed stage that disagrees with the Camera LOP. An UNKNOWN result
reports no frames as sampled and no clearance.

**Clearance, and what it is measured against.** Clearance is the distance from
the lens to the nearest point. When the stage has Gaussian splats it is measured
to splat centres; otherwise to mesh vertices. `against` chooses splats, mesh or
none. The result names the prims it measured, how many points, and at which
frame the geometry was read. It also lists what it left out: mesh prims when
splats were measured, prims that move during the shot, shapes that are not a
mesh, and point-instancer instances. The result gives distances and directions.
It does not say what the lens came close to.

**The path, drawn.** `synapse_spatial_trail` draws the measured path in the
scene as one guide curve, with a short tick every tenth sample showing where
the lens points. SYNAPSE writes one Python Script LOP itself and splices it in
above the node, through `synapse_solaris_build_graph`. The tool takes no code
and no coordinates. The display flag does not move, the curve is `proxy`
purpose, which Karma's default render settings leave out, and the whole change
is one undo step. The handler reads the curve back from the stage, and takes
its node back out if the curve is not there. Asking again changes nothing. A
changed move redraws the same node. The curve is a snapshot of the move when it
was drawn.

**One click from the panel.** The footer has a new control, **Spatial**. A
click, or `/spatial` from the composer, runs `synapse_spatial_trail` from the
panel itself, and no model request is made. The answer is the tool's own
sentence, then what the clearance left out, then how to undo it. It is signed
as the camera-path tool's, with the words "no model request". A click that drew
or redrew the path leaves a "1 CHANGE" receipt, and **REVERT** undoes that one
step. A click that changed nothing leaves no receipt. A click while a task is
running is refused, with the reason. The click enters the model's history as
one ask-and-answer pair, with a note that the answer describes the scene when
it ran.

**Identify, Spatial and Render share the footer's last row.** Identify moves
from the top of the panel to the footer. The three controls sit in one row,
each on a column of the grid above it, and the dock is no taller than before.
`/identify` and `/render` are unchanged.

**A turn that draws the path leaves a receipt.** The turn receipt credits a
tool as a change by keywords in its name. `synapse_spatial_trail` matched none
of them, so a turn that drew the path showed no receipt and no REVERT. The
trail and `synapse_solaris_assemble_chain` now earn credit, and a test pins
every build tool the panel agent may call in its default mode against the
keyword list.

**The spatial lane opens for exactly two tools.** The lane's stage queries had
been kept unregistered. These two tools are now registered, which takes the
registry from 137 to 139 tools. `describe`, `classify` and `frustum` stay
unregistered, and a test that runs on stock CI now pins that. The panel agent
may call the trail in its default mode, because it is one undo-grouped call
whose inputs carry no code.

**Source release for Houdini 22.0.400 / Python 3.13.** No new Windows Setup is
included. The v5.86.0 Setup contains none of the changes in v5.87.0 through
this release. Follow
[source installation](../getting-started/installation.md#source-installation),
then restart Houdini to load the updated source.

## Validation and limits

**The read, live.** In hython 22.0.400, on a copy of the demo scene, the read returned an arc of radius 6.00 m through 20.0 degrees, with 2.09 m of travel at a stage height of 1.29 m. The nearest splat centre came to 1.04 m, left of the lens, at frame 1, measured over 500,000 points. The call took 0.67 s and returned about 1,450 characters. The Camera LOP's parameters matched the composed stage to 0.0 mm and 0.0 degrees, checked with the playhead at frame 1 and again at frame 60. The scene, the frame and the undo stack were unchanged afterwards.

**UNKNOWN, live.** With a node after the camera that adds a transform operation, the read returned UNKNOWN with that reason, no sampled frames and no clearance. The trail refused to draw, built nothing and left no undo entry.

**The trail, live.** The trail added one node between `demo_cam` and `demo_settings`, and a 144-point curve at `/guides/demo_cam_path`. The display flag stayed on `demo_settings`. Asking again changed nothing. A request for every fifth frame redrew the same node with 31 points. Two undos restored the starting scene. A write that was forced to fail was taken back out, with the node gone and the wiring restored. Through the panel's execution bridge with its undo wrapping forced on, the trail was one undo entry, and one undo removed it.

**Mesh clearance, live.** With the world's collider mesh referenced into the stage, `against: mesh` measured the nearest mesh vertex at 1.29 m, below the lens, at frame 1, over 24,643 vertices.

**The trail and Karma, rendered.** Frame 1 was rendered three times with Karma, at 320 × 180, from a camera placed to look at the path, with the world hidden so that only the trail could differ. Without the trail and with the trail as the tool draws it, the two images were identical. With the same trail switched to the default purpose, 6.5% of the pixels changed. So Karma leaves the trail out because of its purpose, and the check could have shown otherwise.

**Through the panel agent, headless.** Four requests each ran three times as one panel turn in hython 22.0.400, on a fresh copy of the demo scene, with the worker built as the panel builds it, on deepseek-v4.1-flash:cloud through Ollama. The four were: draw the path; how close does the camera come; the same question with a node downstream that makes the move UNKNOWN; and draw the path after a Scatter Instances build. All twelve did what was asked. Eleven used one tool call. One UNKNOWN turn made two further read-only calls to look for the cause.
- The trail turns took 3 to 4 s and the read turns about 3 s.
- Every reply that gave numbers gave the ones the tool returned.
- All three UNKNOWN replies said UNKNOWN and gave the tool's reason.
- The section boxes that the Scatter build had drawn were unchanged after the trail, in all three runs.
- A fifth request, worded without the camera's name or the word "draw" ("Show me where this camera travels, and how close it gets to anything."), ran six more times, three of them after a Scatter build. All six drew the path in one tool call, in 3 to 5 s.

**The spoken request, in the GUI, recorded.** One recorded test ran in Houdini 22.0.400 with the panel, on the demo scene, on 2 October, before the Spatial control was built: three turns on deepseek-v4.1-flash:cloud, 10 minutes 55 seconds of recording. It was driven through the GUI by the build harness, with no artist at the keyboard. The scene file's sha256 was unchanged afterwards, and no permission prompt appeared.
- The prompt "Show me where this camera travels, and how close it gets to anything." drew the path in one `synapse_spatial_trail` call, 0.71 s in the tool. The reply gave the tool's numbers. No reply and no tool result named the vendor or a file path.
- One node appeared between `demo_cam` and `demo_settings`, and the display flag did not move.
- With the viewport on Houdini VK in a free perspective view, the path showed as one curve with ticks, coloured from the first frame to the last.
- Edit > Undo removed the curve and its node in one step, and did so again after a redraw.
- The test found no turn receipt after a trail turn, which this release fixes. The rest of what it found is listed under Limits: a wrong answer to a repeat ask after an undo, where the path shows, and the undo step's name.

**The Spatial control, in the GUI, recorded.** A second recorded check ran the same day, on the same scene, with the row of three loaded: 5 minutes 49 seconds of recording, driven the same way. It ran the code before it was committed, and the committed tree is identical to the one that ran. The scene file's sha256 was unchanged afterwards.

**Limits.**
- **Scope of the runs.** The agent runs were headless. The panel was seen in the GUI in the two recorded checks above, and the build harness drove both; no artist has run it at the panel yet. Everything ran on one model, deepseek-v4.1-flash:cloud, and on one scene.
- **The receipt after a spoken turn is tested, not yet seen.** A turn that draws the path now earns a receipt and REVERT, and tests pin that. In the GUI it was seen for a click on Spatial, whose receipt takes a different route, and not for a spoken turn.
- **The receipt credits tools by name.** A turn's receipt counts a tool as a change when its name carries a known keyword. The build tools the panel agent may call are pinned by a test. Of the other changing tools it may call, 30 carry no keyword, so a turn that ran only those shows no receipt and no REVERT. For those, the way back is Houdini's own undo, as it was before this release.
- **A receipt stays after a hand undo.** After the artist's own undo, the click's receipt stays on screen. In the second check REVERT then refused, changed nothing and said so.
- **After an undo, ask for a redraw.** In the first GUI test the path was undone and the question was then repeated. The model answered from the read-only tool, its own check for the curve failed, and the reply said the path was still in the scene. It was not. "Redraw the path." then redrew it in one call. The read does not report whether a path is drawn. A click on Spatial writes its own line into the model's history, with a note that it describes the scene when it ran. Whether that note prevents the wrong answer was not tested. In the second check the model answered a different follow-up question from that line, with no tool call.
- **Where the path shows.** It was seen in a free perspective view on Houdini VK. Through the camera it describes, the path starts at the lens and lies outside the frame. A Karma XPU viewport in a free view was not tried.
- **The undo step's name.** Houdini's Edit menu names the step `SYNAPSE: synapse_spatial_trail: {}`: the tool's internal name and its empty arguments.
- **The model's wording.** The numbers in the replies were the tool's. The sentences around them were the model's own, and three of the nine replies that gave numbers said something the tool did not return. One called the distance steady across the shot, which the tool does not measure. One said the clearance was measured at a single frame; the camera is measured at every sampled frame, and it is the geometry that is read once. One described the robust minimum as ignoring the ten closest points; it is the distance to the tenth-nearest point. In the first GUI test all three replies gave the tool's numbers. Read against the tool's results, nothing in the first or the third reply contradicts them; the second is the case under "After an undo, ask for a redraw".
- **The check is at one frame.** A later node whose extra motion is exactly zero at the current frame, and which adds no transform operation of its own, is the case the read cannot see.
- **The camera has to come from one Camera LOP.** A camera referenced from a file, or animated above the Camera LOP, comes back UNKNOWN instead of measured.
- **Clearance is to points, not surfaces.** Splat extents are not included, and the surface between mesh vertices can be closer than the nearest vertex. Geometry is read where it is at the current frame. Geometry that moves during the shot is counted, not followed.
- **Splats first.** When the stage has splats, the default leaves meshes out and says so. `against: mesh` measures them.
- **The trail is a snapshot.** It does not follow later edits to the camera until it is asked for again.
- **The trail takes about a second.** In the demo scene the trail call ran for about 1.1 s on Houdini's main thread. From the Spatial control, the first click spent 1.3 s in the tool.
- **A withdrawn trail leaves an undo entry.** When a failed write is taken back out, the undo stack keeps one entry that changes nothing.
- **The move class.** It is SYNAPSE's own convention and is labelled as derived. With fewer than five samples no class is given.
- **A reason worded for a model.** On an empty scene the Spatial control's reason ends with "Pass a LOP node path", which an artist at a control cannot do.
- **A narrow dock.** At 340 pixels wide the three controls stay on one row, and after a click most of the answer needs a scroll. That was measured on the real panel widgets outside Houdini. A dock that narrow was not seen in Houdini.
- **The panel tests on real Qt.** They are not part of stock CI, and some fail before and after this release. On PySide6 6.8.3 in a Linux sandbox, 564 passed and 12 failed on this code, against 529 and 15 before the row of three, and none of the 12 is new. Under hython on the workstation, with other test jobs sharing the machine, 580 passed and 33 failed, against 547 and 34. Three tests differed between those two runs. Run alone, three times each, all three passed every time on this code. One of them, the welcome text on a narrow dock at the largest text size, failed every time before the row of three. It passes now because the top of the panel is one control shorter.

The inherited [v5.92.1 limits](v5.92.1.md#validation-and-limits) remain.
